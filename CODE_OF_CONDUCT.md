# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Seli CLI SDK to be a welcoming project for everyone. Everyone who participates — whether as a contributor, maintainer, or user — is expected to treat others with respect and professionalism.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and patient with other contributors, especially newcomers
- Give constructive feedback focused on the code or idea, not the person
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Respect differing opinions and experiences
- Credit others' work and ideas

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruptive behavior in issues, PRs, or discussions

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
